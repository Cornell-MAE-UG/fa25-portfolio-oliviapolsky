While working at a local ice cream shop, I noticed the custom cake and order process was running entirely on paper tickets and word of mouth between the two decorators. Orders got missed, timelines were unclear, and there was no record of what had been promised to a customer.

I built a standalone digital order tracking system to fix this: a Node and Express backend paired with a Vite and React frontend, so orders, statuses, and deadlines live in one place instead of a stack of sticky notes.

Two features I am most proud of:
- Automatic SMS notifications through the Twilio API, so both decorators get pinged the moment a new order comes in or a deadline is approaching, with no manual handoff needed
- A photo scan feature that auto populates order fields from a photo of the handwritten ticket, cutting down on repetitive data entry

The system is fully built with a step by step README for setup, and I am now working on pitching it to my manager for real deployment in the shop. This project is a good example of how I look for the unglamorous, unowned workflow problem and just build the tool to fix it, without waiting to be asked.
