Common Cart is a mobile app I designed and am building to solve a problem every group of roommates has: coordinating shared groceries and splitting the cost fairly.

The core differentiator is a claim mechanic that lets roommates flag which items on a shared list they are picking up and paying for, removing the ambiguity and awkward money conversations that usually come with group shopping.

I led this project end to end. I wrote the PRD, ran early discovery interviews to validate the problem, and built a working prototype in React Native and Expo. It is both a real product I use with my own roommates and a case study in how I approach product work: starting from a lived problem, defining the smallest useful mechanic that solves it, and validating with real users before scaling scope.
